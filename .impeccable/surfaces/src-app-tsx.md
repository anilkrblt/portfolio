# Portfolio home (single page)

## Scope and mode

The whole single-page portfolio (`src/App.tsx`, `src/index.css`, `index.html`).
Visitor mode: **Persuade**. A recruiter decides whether to shortlist and acts by
downloading the CV or making contact.

## Audience, job, action, proof, constraints

- Audience: recruiters and HR screeners, ~30-second scan, many tabs open.
- Job: confirm identity, role and availability; see proof of shipping; leave with the CV
  or an email address.
- Primary action: CV download in the visitor's language (working file link), with email
  one step away. Secondary: open the live product.
- Proof: TranslateYourPDF live; EAA Monitor and Randevai real builds with screenshots;
  the CI-backed development loop table.
- Constraints (confirmed again 2026-11-02): bilingual TR/EN; day/night theme switch;
  WebGL page-curl demo with flat fallback; portrait; project archive; honest status;
  WCAG AA; reduced motion. Motion level: balanced (one strong opening, measured elsewhere).

## Chosen direction and memorable moment

Ebru Teknesi (Turkish paper marbling), chosen by the user on 2026-11-02, replacing the
spy-dossier case file. The memorable moment: pigment drops fall into the indigo bath one
after another, each pushing the last outward into rings around the portrait, then the
stylus swirls the rings into a nightingale's nest; the visitor can comb the bath with the
pointer.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The portfolio is an ebru pulled from the bath: an idea dropped as pigment,
combed into pattern, printed onto paper once, the same one-way trip from idea to live
product. It refuses the dark developer card grid, the warm-paper editorial page and the
case-file it replaces.

OWN-WORLD: Committed çivit indigo (bath) carrying the hero, the demo and the contact;
cool sized-paper ground with indigo ink for reading; earth pigments (ochre, turquoise,
rose, white) live only inside marbling; aşı red means state alone (live, available,
active). Drops are the one shape: status is a drop, the portrait sits in a drop. One
family, Bricolage Grotesque, across optical sizes and widths. Every product sheet has its
own marbled head strip, computed, never an image.

STORY: The recruiter meets name, face, role and availability in the bath, finds the live
product with its touchable demo, two real builds marked in development, the procedure as
